About us

Trusted name in auto electricals

For decades, GS-ASCOT has focused on reliable electrical components for passenger cars, commercial vehicles, and earthmoving equipment.

We manufacture and distribute a wide range of self-starter and alternator assemblies, armatures, regulators, rectifiers, wiper systems, fan motors, lighting, horns, and accessory switches—each line developed to meet OE fitment and durability expectations.

Our catalog is organised for fast lookup by application and part number, so your team can quote and fulfil orders without guesswork.
